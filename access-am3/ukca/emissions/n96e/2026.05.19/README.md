### Licensing/restrictions

- and UM v7.3 simulations. These datasets have no licence information and as such are not available to share or otherwise modify without explicit preapproval.

## Data and Files

### File List

**Data from the Community Emissions Data System (CEDS) for Historical Emissions**
- BC_biofuel_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of black carbon from biofuel.
- BC_fossil_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of black carbon from fossil fuel.
- OC_biofuel_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of organic carbon from biofuel, expressed as carbon
- OC_fossil_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of organic carbon from fossil fuel, expressed as carbon
- SO2_all_low_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of SO2
- SO2_high_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: High-level emissions of SO2
- SO2_low_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Low-level emissions of SO2

**Data from the VUA-CMIP-BB4CMIP6-1-2 model**
- BC_biomass_high_1849_2015_time_series.nc: High-level emissions of black carbon from biomass burning.
- BC_biomass_low_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Low-level emissions of black carbon from biomass burning.
- CO_biomass_low_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of organic carbon from biomass burning.
- OC_biomass_high_1849_2015_time_series.nc: High-level emissions of organic carbon from biomass burning, expressed as carbon
- OC_biomass_low_1849_2015_time_series.nc: Surface emissions of organic carbon from biomass burning, expressed as carbon

**Data from the UM model version 7.3**
- ukca_emiss_Monoterp.nc: Monoterpene surface emissions, expressed as carbon
- ukca_emiss_SO2_nat.nc: SO2 natural emissions
- DMS_land_spiro1992.nc: tendency of atmospheric dimethyl sulfide expressed as sulfur due to emission

**Synthetic data**
- dummy_SO2_high_anthropogenic_1849_2015_time_series.nc: dummy zero emission for high-level SO2 emissions

All files are netcdf files with metadata.
